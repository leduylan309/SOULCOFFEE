At Spark, we believe learning should continue beyond the classroom. Our Resource Library provides practical tools designed to help parents support reading, writing, vocabulary, and literacy development at home.

Whether your child is learning phonics, building reading confidence, or preparing for future academic success, these resources can help reinforce learning through simple, structured practice. We are continually expanding our library and adding new resources throughout the year.
