Every student begins at a different point in their learning journey.

Whether your child is learning to read, developing confidence in English, strengthening academic writing skills, or preparing for IELTS, Spark offers structured pathways designed to support long-term success.

Explore our programs below to find the best fit for your child's goals and current level.

01

Phonics & Literacy Development

Building Strong Foundations for Reading Success

Our Phonics & Literacy Development Program helps students develop the essential skills needed for reading, writing, spelling, and comprehension. Through a structured literacy approach, students progress from sound awareness and early decoding to fluent reading and advanced literacy skills.

Key Focus Areas

  • Phonics and decoding
  • Reading fluency
  • Spelling development
  • Writing foundations
  • Vocabulary growth
  • Reading comprehension
Ideal forStudents beginning their reading journey or those who need stronger literacy foundations.

04

Tides: Upper-Primary English Program

Building Confident, Independent English Learners

Tides is an upper-primary English class designed for learners working at CEFR A2. Students build confidence across reading, writing, listening, and speaking while exploring engaging real-world topics including community, the environment, science, and global issues.

As students strengthen their grammar and expand their vocabulary, they begin using English more independently through discussions, projects, and structured writing tasks. Tides helps learners progress from basic communication toward greater fluency and prepares them for more advanced academic English in the future.

Key Focus Areas

  • Reading comprehension
  • Structured writing
  • Listening skills
  • Speaking confidence
  • Grammar development
  • Vocabulary growth
  • Discussion and project work
  • Real-world and global topics
Ideal forUpper-primary learners working at CEFR A2 who are ready to build greater independence and fluency in English.

06

Speak Clearly Program

Building Clear, Confident Communicators

Speak Clearly is an 10-week pronunciation, articulation, fluency, and oral communication program for children ages 5–13. Through engaging, structured lessons, students develop clearer speech, stronger listening skills, expressive language, reading fluency, and the confidence to communicate in everyday and academic settings.

Key Focus Areas

  • Pronunciation and articulation
  • Speech clarity and fluency
  • Listening and conversation skills
  • Vocabulary and expressive language
  • Reading fluency and comprehension
  • Storytelling and presentation skills
Ideal forNative English speakers and English language learners who want to speak more clearly, communicate with greater confidence, and strengthen their oral language skills.
