Every international school has its own curriculum, expectations, and academic challenges. However, one thing remains consistent across all schools: students need strong reading, writing, vocabulary, and critical thinking skills to succeed.

At Spark, we work with students from a wide range of international schools throughout Ho Chi Minh City. While each school has unique requirements, many students face similar challenges related to literacy, academic English, and written communication. This guide outlines the common expectations and challenges students encounter and explains how Spark supports their academic development.

FAQs

International school support, academic English, reading, writing and assessments

Does Spark follow the curriculum used by my child’s school?

Spark does not teach a school’s curriculum directly. We focus on the literacy, reading, writing, vocabulary, and critical thinking skills that support success across a wide range of international curricula — helping students perform more confidently in English, Science, Humanities, and other subjects regardless of school.

Can Spark help students from BIS, ISHCMC, AIS, Vinschool, and Asian School?

Yes. We regularly work with students from a variety of international and bilingual schools throughout HCMC. Although each school has unique expectations, students often face similar challenges in reading comprehension, writing, academic vocabulary, and critical thinking.

My child already speaks English fluently. Why might they still need support?

Conversational English and academic English are not the same. Many students communicate confidently in everyday situations but struggle with reading complex texts, writing essays, understanding academic vocabulary, analyzing information, and supporting ideas with evidence.

How does Spark support students in the IB program?

IB students are expected to think critically, conduct research, reflect on learning, and communicate ideas clearly. Spark develops reading comprehension, analytical thinking, academic writing, vocabulary, and discussion skills that support students across IB subjects and year levels.

Can Spark help with school writing assignments?

Yes. We provide both academic writing instruction and tutoring support for essays, reports, reading responses and research projects. Our goal is not to complete the work for students but to help them organize ideas, structure paragraphs and essays, develop arguments, use academic vocabulary, and edit and revise — becoming confident, independent writers.

My child struggles with reading comprehension. Can Spark help?

Yes. Students may read words accurately but still struggle to understand meaning, identify key ideas, make inferences, analyze information, or explain what they have read. Our literacy-focused approach strengthens both accuracy and deeper comprehension.

What if my child dislikes reading?

Many reluctant readers simply have not yet developed confidence. We build it through appropriate text selection, structured literacy instruction, vocabulary development, reading success experiences, and positive encouragement.

Does Spark help students prepare for secondary school?

Yes. Academic expectations increase significantly at the transition: more complex texts, longer responses, larger workloads, more critical thinking. Our programs help students develop the skills for a successful transition.

Can Spark help students who are already performing well?

Absolutely. Many high-performing students attend Spark to expand vocabulary, improve writing sophistication, develop analytical skills, and prepare for future academic challenges.

How does Spark assess students?

We begin with an assessment of reading ability, comprehension, vocabulary, writing, speaking, and academic English skills — depending on age and level — to recommend the most appropriate learning pathway.

How often is progress monitored?

Continuously, through classroom observations, reading assessments, writing samples, vocabulary development, and ongoing performance reviews.

Does Spark support students who are behind — or ahead of — grade level?

Yes to both. Students closing literacy gaps receive targeted, personalized support. Advanced students are stretched with increasingly sophisticated texts, vocabulary, and academic tasks.

Does Spark prepare students for IELTS?

Yes. Our IELTS Success Program develops academic writing, reading comprehension, vocabulary, listening, critical thinking, and examination strategies — genuine language ability alongside exam preparation.

How long does it take to see progress?

Every student develops at a different pace. Many families notice improvements in confidence and engagement within the first few months; more significant literacy and academic gains develop over longer periods of consistent learning.

What makes Spark different from a traditional English center?

Many programs focus on conversational English or short-term exam prep. Spark focuses on literacy, academic language, reading, writing, vocabulary and critical thinking — helping students use English to learn, think, communicate, and succeed academically.

Which Spark program is right for my child?

It depends on age, current level, strengths, and goals. Our assessment identifies the right pathway: Phonics & Literacy, ESL, Academic English & Writing, or IELTS Success.
