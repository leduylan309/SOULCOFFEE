Tides is an upper-primary English class designed for learners working at CEFR A2. The program helps students build confidence across reading, writing, listening, and speaking while exploring meaningful real-world topics such as community, the environment, science, and global issues.

At this stage, students move beyond basic communication and begin using English more independently. Through discussions, projects, and structured writing tasks, learners strengthen their grammar, expand their vocabulary, and develop the skills needed to express ideas clearly and confidently.

Tides prepares students for the transition toward more advanced academic English by helping them become thoughtful, capable, and independent communicators.

Why This Stage Matters

Upper-primary is a critical stage in language development. Students at CEFR A2 have developed basic communication skills, but they need structured support to:

  • Use English more independently
  • Communicate ideas in greater detail
  • Read and understand longer texts
  • Write structured paragraphs
  • Build academic vocabulary
  • Participate confidently in discussions
  • Think critically about real-world topics

Tides provides the bridge between foundational English and the academic English skills students will need in later years.

The Spark Approach to Upper-Primary English

Tides combines structured instruction with meaningful communication. Students develop language skills through engaging topics, collaborative learning, and purposeful tasks that encourage them to use English actively.

Lessons focus on:

  • Reading comprehension
  • Structured writing
  • Listening skills
  • Speaking confidence
  • Grammar development
  • Vocabulary growth
  • Discussion and project work
  • Real-world and global topics

Students are encouraged to ask questions, share opinions, explain ideas, and connect learning to the world around them.

Who Is This Program For?

Tides is ideal for:

  • Upper-primary learners working at CEFR A2
  • Students ready to build greater independence in English
  • Learners who need stronger reading and writing skills
  • Students preparing for more advanced academic English
  • Learners who benefit from engaging, topic-based learning

The Spark Approach to Upper-Primary English

Reading Development

Students read a variety of fiction and non-fiction texts connected to real-world themes. They learn to:

  • Identify main ideas and details
  • Make inferences
  • Understand new vocabulary in context
  • Summarize information
  • Respond to texts thoughtfully

Writing Development

Students learn to organize ideas clearly and write with greater structure and accuracy. Writing tasks include:

  • Descriptive paragraphs
  • Opinion writing
  • Informational writing
  • Short reports
  • Project-based writing

Speaking and Listening

Students build confidence through discussions, presentations, and collaborative projects. They practice:

  • Expressing opinions
  • Asking and answering questions
  • Explaining ideas
  • Listening for key information
  • Participating in group discussions

Grammar and Vocabulary

Grammar and vocabulary are taught in context so students understand how language works in real communication. Students develop:

  • Accurate sentence structure
  • Expanding vocabulary
  • Connecting ideas with linking words
  • Using tenses correctly
  • Greater fluency and precision

Critical Thinking

Students explore global topics that encourage them to think about the world around them. They learn to:

  • Compare ideas
  • Form opinions
  • Support ideas with reasons
  • Solve problems
  • Make connections across topics

Learning Through Meaningful Topics

Tides uses real-world themes to make learning relevant and engaging. Topics include:

  • Community and culture
  • The environment
  • Science and discovery
  • Global issues
  • Health and wellbeing
  • Technology
  • People and places

Through these topics, students build knowledge while developing the language to discuss, explain, and write about the world around them.

What Progress Looks Like

As students progress through Tides, parents often notice:

  • Greater confidence speaking English
  • Longer and more detailed responses
  • Improved reading comprehension
  • More organized writing
  • Expanded vocabulary
  • Better grammar accuracy
  • Increased independence
  • Stronger participation in discussions

The Spark Difference

Tides is more than an English class. It is a structured pathway that helps upper-primary learners grow into confident, independent communicators.

Students are encouraged to:

  • Think critically
  • Communicate clearly
  • Read and write with purpose
  • Explore ideas about the world
  • Take ownership of their learning

Our goal is to help students move from basic communication toward fluency and academic readiness.
