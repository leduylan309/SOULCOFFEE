Program Overview

Our Phonics & Literacy Development Program helps students build the essential skills needed for reading, writing, spelling, and comprehension. Using a structured literacy approach, students learn how sounds, letters, and words work together to create meaning.

Students progress through carefully sequenced levels that develop:

  • Phonemic awareness
  • Decoding and blending skills
  • Spelling patterns
  • Reading fluency
  • Vocabulary development
  • Reading comprehension
  • Writing foundations

This program is designed to help students become confident, independent readers and writers.

Why Phonics Matters

Strong phonics skills are the foundation of literacy success. Students who understand how sounds and letters work together are able to:

  • Read unfamiliar words accurately
  • Spell with confidence
  • Read fluently and independently
  • Understand what they read
  • Develop stronger writing skills

Without strong phonics foundations, students often rely on guessing or memorization, which limits long-term reading growth.

Program Levels

Level 1: Phonics Foundations

Students begin developing phonemic awareness and letter-sound knowledge.

Skills Developed

  • Letter recognition
  • Letter sounds
  • Sound blending
  • Early decoding
  • Simple CVC words

Outcome

Students begin reading simple words and short sentences.

Level 2: Decoding & Blending

Students strengthen decoding skills and begin reading with greater accuracy.

Skills Developed

  • Consonant blends
  • Digraphs
  • Word families
  • Sight words
  • Early fluency

Outcome

Students read simple texts with increasing confidence.

Level 3: Reading Fluency

Students develop smoother, more expressive reading and expand vocabulary.

Skills Developed

  • Long vowel patterns
  • Multisyllabic words
  • Fluency development
  • Vocabulary building
  • Sentence comprehension

Outcome

Students read longer texts fluently and understand key ideas.

Level 4: Spelling & Writing Development

Students apply phonics knowledge to spelling and structured writing.

Skills Developed

  • Advanced spelling patterns
  • Word structure
  • Sentence writing
  • Paragraph organization
  • Grammar foundations

Outcome

Students write clear sentences and organized paragraphs.

Level 5: Advanced Literacy Skills

Students build stronger comprehension and independent reading skills.

Skills Developed

  • Reading comprehension strategies
  • Inference and analysis
  • Academic vocabulary
  • Independent reading
  • Extended writing

Outcome

Students read and write confidently across a range of texts.

FAQs

Common questions about our Phonics & Literacy Development Program

What age is this program for?

The program supports early readers from age 3 upward, as well as older students who need stronger literacy foundations. Placement is based on reading ability, not age.

How long does it take to complete each level?

Progress varies by student. Most students complete a level within one to two terms, depending on attendance, practice, and starting point.

What phonics method do you use?

We use systematic, evidence-based phonics instruction aligned with Cambridge Young Learners standards, teaching children to decode English through blending, segmenting, and reading connected text.

My child can already read some words. Do they still need phonics?

Often, yes. Many children memorize words without understanding how sounds and letters work together. Our assessment identifies gaps in decoding and spelling that limit long-term reading growth.

How do parents track progress?

Parents receive regular updates through ClassDojo and quarterly parent meetings, with clear information about reading accuracy, fluency, spelling, and comprehension.
