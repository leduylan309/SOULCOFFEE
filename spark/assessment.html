Why Take an Assessment?

Every child learns differently. Our assessment helps us understand your child's current English level, confidence, and learning needs so we can recommend the most suitable class.

We focus on:

  • Speaking ability
  • Pronunciation
  • Reading skills
  • Phonics knowledge
  • Listening comprehension
  • Grammar foundations
  • Confidence and communication

Our goal is not to "test" children — it is to help them feel comfortable, supported, and ready to grow.

FAQs

We understand that parents may have questions before booking an assessment. Here are some of the most common questions about our assessment process, class placement, and student support.

How long does the assessment take?

Most assessments take around 30–45 minutes, depending on the student's age and English level. During this time, our teachers guide students through speaking, reading, phonics, and listening activities in a calm and supportive environment.

Does my child need to prepare?

No preparation is needed before the assessment. We want children to feel relaxed and comfortable so we can see their natural English ability and confidence level during the session.

Will parents receive feedback?

Yes. After the assessment, parents receive personalized feedback about their child's strengths, areas for improvement, and recommended class placement. Our team is also happy to answer questions and discuss the next steps for learning.

Is the assessment stressful?

Not at all. Our teachers create a friendly and encouraging environment designed to help students feel comfortable and confident. The activities are interactive and supportive rather than formal or intimidating.

What ages do you assess?

We assess a wide range of learners, including young children, teenagers, and adult students. Each assessment is adjusted based on the student's age, learning needs, and current English level.

What skills are included in the assessment?

Our assessments may include speaking, pronunciation, reading, phonics, listening, grammar, and comprehension activities. The exact activities are adjusted based on the student's age and English level.

How will I know which class is right for my child?

After the assessment, our teachers recommend a class based on your child's current ability, confidence level, and learning needs. We aim to place students in an environment where they can feel supported while continuing to grow.

Can shy or nervous students still complete the assessment?

Absolutely. Many students feel shy at first, especially when speaking English with a new teacher. Our teachers are experienced in helping children feel relaxed, encouraged, and comfortable throughout the session.

Is the assessment online or in person?

Assessments may be offered in person or online depending on availability and student needs. Our team will help you choose the option that is most convenient and comfortable for your child.

What happens after the assessment?

After the assessment, our team will discuss the results with you and recommend the most suitable class for your child. We will also explain your child's strengths, areas for improvement, and the next steps to help them continue building confidence in English.
